Diction for this one: precise and faintly technical, almost bureaucratic — the register of a status report, a manual, or a form. Exact quantities, plain nouns, no ornament. The mood comes from applying that flat vocabulary to something it has no business describing.

Imagery ban: never reach for the small-poetic-particle image. No dust, no motes, no grains of sand, no crumbs, no specks suspended in a shaft of light, no ants or insects, no gears or clockwork. Do not give inanimate things a breath or a heartbeat, and do not build similes out of weather — rain on a roof, storms, heat rising from the ground. All of these are exhausted; if one occurs to you, that is the signal it is the wrong one. Reach for something concrete, specific and modern instead.
